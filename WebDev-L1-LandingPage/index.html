What's On The Wall

Routes for every session

Every program is tagged by difficulty the same way a route-setter tapes a hold — so you always know what you're walking into.

V0–V2

First Traverse

A 90-minute intro to movement, footwork, and falling safely. No experience or gear needed — shoes and chalk included.

5.6–5.8

Top-Rope Basics

Belay certification in one session. Learn the knot, the catch, and the communication that keeps every climb safe.

V3–V5

Movement Lab

A weekly small-group session on flagging, heel hooks, and reading a problem before you touch the wall.

5.10–5.11

Youth Team

Structured training for climbers 8–17, built around competition prep and long-term technique.

V6–V8

The Board Room

Open access to our adjustable training board — kilter-style, angle-tunable, for climbers chasing a specific grade.

5.12+

Setter's Circuit

Our hardest lines, reset monthly by the same crew that sets for regional comps. Limited to members.

The Space

Built by climbers, for the next hold

Anchorpoint started in a converted warehouse with one wall and a handful of regulars trading beta over coffee. Six years later, the warehouse is still here — bare concrete, steel trusses, and a wall that gets reset every week by setters who climb it themselves.

We kept the things that mattered: a community board where people actually talk to each other, a training room that doesn't feel like an afterthought, and a front desk that remembers your name by your second visit.
